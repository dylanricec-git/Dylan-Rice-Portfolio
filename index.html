About

I'm a marketing and content professional with a background in communication, social media strategy, and live event coordination. I graduated from UMass Amherst with a B.A. in Communication and a Theater minor, and I've spent my career finding ways to make brands feel like people, including a viral video that landed on Barstool Sports and ESPN. I do voiceover for my own content, which means I don't just write the script, I deliver it too.

Music has always been part of how I work. I play bass and sing in Off the Coast, a band that gigs across New England, and I spent time in an a cappella group somewhere along the way too. The best part of playing live is watching people actually have fun, being in the room, in the moment, not thinking about anything else. That's what I want to create for people, whether it's a campaign, an event, or a video. I'll figure out whatever the project needs to make that happen.
